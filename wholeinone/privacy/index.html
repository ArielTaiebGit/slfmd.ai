Whole in One — Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Short version: your lists stay on your phone. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What stays on your phone

Everything you add (items, people, ratings, notes, watched episodes, settings, an imported Netflix file) is stored only on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it. You can export a copy from Settings › Export my data.

What leaves your phone

When you search for a film, show, game or book, the words you typed are sent directly from the app to public catalogs to find the title, its cover and details: The Movie Database (TMDB), TVmaze, the Steam store (games), Wikipedia / Wikidata and Open Library. As with any internet request, these services receive your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply. Covers are loaded from those services' image servers. Nothing else is sent: not your lists, not your contacts, not who you are.

iCloud sync

If iCloud is on for your iPhone, the app keeps a copy of your lists in your own iCloud account, in a private area that only Whole in One on your devices can read, so your lists appear on your other iPhones and iPads signed into the same Apple ID. This copy is stored by Apple under your Apple ID; we have no access to it and no server of our own. You can switch it off in Settings › iCloud sync; deleting the app's data in iCloud settings removes the copy.

Sagas and show status

To show which films, shows, games and books belong to the same saga, and whether a show has ended, the app sends the catalog identifiers of titles in your lists (for example the Wikidata number of "Star Wars") to Wikidata, TMDB or TVmaze. It does not send your lists, ratings or notes.

Notifications

If you turn on running-low reminders, the app schedules them on your phone. They are not sent through any server.

Links you open

Buttons such as "Open in IMDb" or "Open Viewing activity" open other websites in your browser. Those sites have their own policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If the app ever adds an account or ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the release notes.
