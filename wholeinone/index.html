Whole in One

All your lists. Type anything, it finds its list.

  • One capture bar. "Remind me to call the bank", "milk and eggs", "Severance", "lent Mario Galaxy to Thierry": each lands in the right list.
  • Films and shows with posters, episodes left, time watched, where they come from, and a catch-up mode.
  • Top 500. The all-time favourite films and games, ranked: tick off what you have seen and played.
  • Sagas. See what is left to watch, play or read in a franchise, in order, and mark a whole saga seen in one tap.
  • Groceries with about 250 everyday basics, sorted by aisle, what is in season, recipes from around the world, a vegetarian or vegan mode, and reminders when something runs low.
  • Trips and places. Pin where you have been and where you want to go, and spin the globe for where next.
  • Gift ideas per person, recommendations and who made them, trips, books, games, and the things you lent.
  • Your language. English, French or Spanish, switched in Settings.
  • Private by design. Free. Your lists sync through your own iCloud on iPhone and are kept in your phone's own backup on Android; shared lists for couples and households are next.

In App Store review now. Follow @slfmd.ai to hear the day it is out. For iPhone, iPhone Duo (folded and unfolded) and Macs with Apple silicon, in English, French and Spanish.

Android coming soon, with the same lists and features. Testing has started: become a tester.
