Whole in One

All your lists. Type anything, it finds its list.

  • One capture bar. "Remind me to call the bank", "milk and eggs", "Severance", "lent Mario Galaxy to Thierry": each lands in the right list.
  • Films and shows with posters, episodes left, time watched, and a catch-up mode.
  • Sagas. See what is left to watch, play or read in a franchise, in order, and mark a whole saga seen in one tap.
  • Groceries with about 240 everyday basics, sorted by aisle, that learn what you buy and remind you when it runs low.
  • Gift ideas per person, recommendations and who made them, trips, books, games, and the things you lent.
  • Your language. English, French or Spanish, switched in Settings.
  • Private by design. Free, no account, no ads, no tracking. Your lists stay on your phone and sync through your own iCloud.

Coming soon on the App Store. iOS only for now: iPhone, iPhone Duo (folded and unfolded) and Macs with Apple silicon. In English, French and Spanish.
