Whole in One — Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: ariel@slfmd.ai. We answer within a few days.

Where is my data?

On your phone, and in your own iCloud when Settings › iCloud sync is on, so it appears on your other iPhones and iPads and comes back if you reinstall. You can also keep a file copy with Settings › Export my data, and restore it with Settings › Restore a backup.

I erased everything by mistake

Erase everything only clears this phone and switches iCloud sync off first. Switch iCloud sync back on in Settings and your lists come back.

A title shows the wrong poster

Open it and tap "Not this one? Change", then pick the right one. Settings › Data sources › Look for missing covers tries again for every cover that did not come up.

Importing Netflix history

On netflix.com: Account › Profile › Viewing activity › Download all. Then in the app: Watch › Import Netflix history.
