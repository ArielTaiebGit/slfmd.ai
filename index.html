Whole in One

AppiPhone · iPhone Duo · Mac · Android soonEnglish, French, Spanish

All your lists in one app. Type anything, it finds its list.

  • One capture bar. "Milk and eggs", "Severance", "lent Mario Galaxy to Thierry": each line lands where it belongs.
  • Films, shows, games, books with covers, episodes left and time watched, sagas, a Top 500 to work through, and a roulette for tonight.
  • Groceries by aisle and by season, with recipes, that learn what you buy.
  • Trips and places on a map: where you have been, where you want to go, and where next.
  • Gift ideas per person, recommendations, to-dos and the things you lent.
  • Free, and private. Your lists stay yours: synced through your own iCloud on iPhone, kept in your phone's own backup on Android, and soon shared with the people you choose.
